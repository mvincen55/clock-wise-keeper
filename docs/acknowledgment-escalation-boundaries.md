# Escalation Product Boundaries

- Routine acknowledgments respect schedules, days off, call-outs, office closures, and quiet hours.
- Serious safety, cash-integrity, compliance, and workplace events use the existing immediate incident/accountability path.
- Blocks, snoozes, and questions are visible states, not silent dismissal mechanisms.
- Escalation status is attached to the work item, not used as a label on the person.
- Acknowledgment does not equal agreement, comprehension, or discipline.
- Owners and managers see operational status and receipts, not private training conversations.
- SMS, native push, DSO administration, and complex multi-location controls are intentionally outside this slice.
