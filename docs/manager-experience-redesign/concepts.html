The manager experience, redesigned around the manager's day

One canonical place to manage each fact, surfaced anywhere it matters. Home becomes the briefing. Management becomes the workbench with four rooms: Attention, People, Payroll, Office. Every row deep-links into the editors the product already has. Nothing that works is rebuilt.

0The five-second read

Here is the office. Here are the few things that need you. Everything else is okay.

The test for every screen below: can a dental office manager open it and know, in five seconds, whether the office is operating normally and what needs her. When the answer is "nothing", the screen says so in one line and gets quieter.

The problem is architecture, not features. The attendance engine, schedules, PTO, approvals, reports, goals, checklists, FOF, Close the Day, forms, training, employee records, settings, permissions, and deep links all work. They were assembled feature by feature, so the manager experience became two command centers, a dozen queues, four renderings of "who is here", and a settings hub of hubs. The manager has to know where a feature lives before she can respond to it.

The fix is ownership. A fact may surface on Home, in Management, on payroll, and on an employee record. It is managed in exactly one place, and every surface deep-links there. Home summarizes; Management acts. Approvals, Team, Team Attendance's unreviewed list, bypass review, acknowledgments, and the rest stop being separate rooms and become one triaged queue with one row shape and one primary action per item.

Quiet intelligence. No zero is an alert. No card exists to fill space. Missing data is narrated, never rendered as zero. Every derived line carries its receipts. Consequence orders everything: people waiting on you, then the record of truth, then follow-through.

"Who is here?" is computed four ways and rendered three ways

What is already right, and is preserved

Home · exact responsibility

"How is the office right now, and what needs me?" A briefing, never a workbench. It renders no forms. Every row is a deep link into the canonical place. It shows the top five Attention items with their inline primary actions, today's exceptions, yesterday in one line, pace in one sentence, the running challenge, and the manager's own lines. When the queue is empty it says so once. After close it turns into Before you leave.

Management · exact responsibility

"Do the management work." The only place actions happen. Attention is the whole queue with a panel that mounts the existing editor for each kind. People is the single live roster plus records. Payroll is readiness before reporting. Office is periodic management and office-wide configuration. No administration grid; no duplicated vitals; no second pulse.

Mobile

Role-shaped bottom navigation: managers get Home · Workplace · Playbook · Inbox · Manage (badge = open attention); members get four tabs. Account, settings, help, privacy lock, and sign out move under the office mark in the header, so the "More" sheet goes away. Manage opens Attention; People, Payroll, and Office are one row of segments. Every item opens a bottom sheet with its evidence and one primary action; a notification deep-links straight to that sheet.

5High-fidelity desktop concepts

The same rows, the same editors, one workbench

Fixture data for a fictional office (Sample Family Dental). Switch scenarios to see the same surfaces on a quiet day, a busy day, and at wrap-up. Rows are clickable; approvals, sign-offs, and the fix-and-return flow work.

Manager Home

The briefing. State line, the top five Attention items with inline actions, today's exceptions, yesterday in one line, pace in one sentence, the running challenge, and the manager's own lines. The clock stays in the header chip. After close it becomes Before you leave.

Management → Attention

One triaged queue for every source: requests, content in review, challenge verification, missing time, unsealed closeouts, tardies, records, bypasses, escalated acknowledgments, overdue training, incidents. Grouped by verb, ordered by consequence. Select a row to open its panel: what happened, why it surfaced, the receipts, one primary action, and the record.

Management → People

Today is the single live roster (phase-aware, exceptions first). Everyone is the roster with today's status, open items, the last 30 days, and the schedule summary. Team Attendance is the existing workspace. Patterns reads repeats against the office's own thresholds, without rankings. Click a person to open their record.

A focused employee record

A stable header, an at-a-glance strip, then sections. Overview holds this person's open items, the last fourteen days, and manager notes. Each section is the canonical view; edits invoke the canonical editors. The schedule editor lives here and nowhere else.

Payroll readiness → fix → return

Pick the period; see the records that make payroll questionable, each with its exact fix. "Fix the day" opens the day in Team Attendance with a return pill; saving returns to Payroll with the row marked fixed and audited. Ready is one line and one button.

Team Attendance

The existing workspace, unchanged in capability: the whole office or one person, every punch of the day, days off, tardies, closures, and row-level editing for anyone. Two additions: it honors a return destination, and its unreviewed list is the same Attention items filtered to this view, so "missing" has one definition.

Management → Office

Periodic management as an index with one live fact per area. No cards, no grid, and no second copy of the application.

6Mobile manager experience

The floor in your pocket, not the desktop shrunk

No performance strip, no notes board, no roster tables. Home is the state line and the top three items. Manage is the queue with a bottom sheet per item. Notifications open the sheet directly. The sticky clock bar stays above the tabs.

8Interaction model

Triage, deep link, disclose, correct and return, stay quiet

Attention triage

Row → panel → act → the row collapses with Done · undo for ten seconds → focus moves to the next row. Keyboard: arrows move, Enter opens, A and D approve or decline where they apply. The panel mounts the product's existing editor for the item's kind; it never reimplements one.

Deep linking

Every row and every notification opens the exact item panel at /management?item=<kind>:<id>. Panels link to the underlying record with the feature's existing deep link (/management/attendance?employee=&date=, /deposit-log?date=&step=, /incident-reports?report=, /training?assignment=, /acknowledgments?assignment=). Editors accept ?return= and show a return pill after saving.

Progressive disclosure

A row is one line and one primary action. The panel adds what happened, why the system surfaced it, the deadline, the receipts, and the history. The record has everything. "Why am I seeing this?" is a disclosure on the panel that names the office rule and the recorded items it read, in the office's words: punches, schedule, Close the Day, never table names.

Correct an issue and return to context

Payroll row → the day in Team Attendance with a return pill at the top → save → the pill returns to Payroll with the row updated and an audit line: Fixed 2 min ago by you. The same pattern serves Attention → editor → Attention. Try it in the Payroll concept above.

Empty and quiet states

Home collapses to the state line, Nothing needs you, and one-line sections. Attention reads Nothing waiting · 2 parked until Friday. Payroll reads Ready. No card appears to fill space, no zero becomes a row, no green floods the page. Switch the Home concept to the quiet scenario to see it.

Office doing well vs. several genuine issues

The same components in both cases. What changes is the count, the verb groupings appearing, and the first item being proposed as step into this first with its receipts. Color appears only on tone dots and the one number that matters. Motion is limited to the row collapse and the return pill, and honors reduced motion.

Data honesty

Missing data is narrated (not recorded), never zero. Pace lines state their day scope. Every rule-derived or AI-derived line carries receipts with sources. There is no office-health score; the office's state is a sentence built from recorded facts, and the facts are one click away.
