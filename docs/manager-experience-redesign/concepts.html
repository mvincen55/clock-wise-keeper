Purple Envelope · design proposal · round 2 · September 2026 Architecture · information design · interaction · workflow

The manager experience, redesigned around the manager's day

One canonical place to manage each fact, surfaced anywhere it matters. Home briefs. Management acts, in four rooms: Attention, People, Payroll, Office. Every row deep-links into the editors the product already has. Round 2 keeps that architecture and makes it simpler, harder-edged, and faster to use.

0The five-second read

Here is the office. Here are the few things that need you. Everything else is okay.

The test for every screen: can a dental office manager open it and know, in five seconds, whether the office is operating normally and what needs her. When the answer is "nothing", the screen says so in one line and gets quieter.

The problem is architecture, not features. The attendance engine, schedules, PTO, approvals, reports, goals, checklists, FOF, Close the Day, forms, training, employee records, settings, and deep links all work. They were assembled feature by feature, so the manager experience became two command centers, a dozen queues, four renderings of "who is here", and a settings hub of hubs.

The fix is ownership, enforced literally. A fact may surface on Home, in Management, on payroll, and on an employee record. It is managed in exactly one place. Home summarizes and only navigates; every consequential action happens in Management. Attention is one queue with a hard admission rule. One derived state powers every count, badge, heading, and button.

The rhythm is Payroll Readiness, everywhere. State → exception → exact action → canonical editor → return → resolved. Attention, People, Close the Day follow-up, policy review, bypass follow-up, and incidents all run on it.

Round 2 · what changed

  • Home lost its buttons. Every row carries one navigation action, Review or Open, that lands on the exact Attention item. Approve, Sign off, Seal, and Fix live only in Management.
  • Busy Home shrank. Top three items, exceptions only for Today, yesterday and pace as two status lines, the challenge only when noteworthy, Mine only when something needs the manager personally.
  • "Step into this first" is gone. Attention is a flat list ordered by consequence; the first row is first.
  • Attention has a hard admission rule, and Inbox, Attention, and notifications have one sentence each.
  • The employee record has five sections, not seven.
  • Undo is gone for business actions. Consequential decisions confirm first and reverse only as an audited action; ten-second undo remains for Park and Snooze.
  • Shared selectors over one set of facts drive the badge, Home, Attention, and Payroll; each count is a labeled subset.
  • Legibility pass: fewer labels, larger secondary text, bigger targets, more room between groups.

Home · exact responsibility

"How is the office right now, and what needs me?" A briefing, never a workbench. One sentence of state built from recorded facts. The top three Attention items, each with a single navigation action, Review or Open, that lands on the exact item in Management. Today shows exceptions and a count, never a roster. Yesterday and pace are two status lines. The running challenge appears only when it is newly launched, needs a decision, is nearing its deadline, hits a milestone, goes off track, or finishes; otherwise it stays in Office. Mine appears only when something needs the manager personally. After close, Needs you becomes Before you leave.

Management · exact responsibility

"Do the management work." The only place actions happen. Attention is the whole queue with a panel that mounts the existing editor for each kind. People is the single live roster plus records. Payroll is readiness before reporting. Office is periodic management and office-wide configuration. No administration grid, no duplicated vitals, no second pulse, no recommendation box above a list that is already in order.

Inbox · Attention · notifications

Inbox is communication from people. Messages, and notes handed to a person (the office-labeled "doctor notes"). Attention is operational work created by records and workflows. Decisions, fixes, rule-triggered follow-ups. Notifications are delivery, never a destination. The bell lists deliveries; each opens an Attention item, an Inbox thread, or a record, and is read when that opens. Nudges leave Inbox: they are system-generated, so each renders on the surface it concerns and the member's own Home counts them. "Request" stops being a category name: PTO, correction, and change requests are Decide items in Attention; the employee's own submissions stay "My requests" in Workplace; the doctor-notes tab drops the word.

5High-fidelity desktop concepts · round 3

Three layers of state, shared selectors, honest counts

A simulated prototype on fixture data. Business facts (punches, days, requests, closeouts, records) live in one layer and change only through a canonical editor. The manager’s work state (needs action, waiting on someone, followed up) and presentation (parked, snoozed) are separate layers. Every count is a labeled subset: needs me now, waiting on others, deferred, all unresolved, payroll issues in the period. Asking, parking, snoozing, or reading never changes a fact. Controls that are illustrative say so.

Manager Home

A sentence of state built from facts, the top three needs-me-now items with one navigation action each, a line for what is waiting on others or deferred (still open), today’s exceptions, two status lines. Why? reveals the pace figures in place. The challenge appears only when noteworthy; Mine only when needed. A stale attendance source is announced, never hidden.

Management → Attention

Four labeled lists over one set of facts: needs me now (a verified coverage or safety issue first, then deadlines, decisions, fixes, follow-ups), waiting on others, deferred, done today. The panel validates required input, confirms consequential writes in one sentence, opens the canonical editor in place with a return pill, and keeps an appended trail per item. Reversals are new events, offered only where the product has or is planned to have an audited path.

Management → People

Today reads facts only: a snoozed reminder never changes a person’s status. Everyone shows unresolved items per person. Patterns is context and never creates an item. Click any person to open their record; full fixture detail exists for Ken W., and the other records say so.

Employee record · five sections

Overview distinguishes “needs action now” from “open, deferred” and “waiting on others”. Time holds a bounded attendance preview with a working jump strip, the schedule (the only editor), and time off. Record holds records, incidents, bypasses, adjustments, and notes, including follow-up notes written from Attention.

Payroll readiness → fix → return

Readiness is computed from the period’s records, never from what is visible in Attention. A day the manager asked about stays listed as waiting · still unresolved for payroll. A stale attendance source turns readiness into can’t confirm with a retry, never into ready. Export is never blocked; unresolved issues print on the report. Fix the day opens the editor with a return pill and comes back here.

Team Attendance

The existing workspace, unchanged in capability, reading the same facts: an asked-about day still shows no clock-out · waiting on Priya until a punch is saved.

Management → Office

Periodic management as an index. Practice performance (targets, pace evidence, and the new Missed appointments import from Dentrix 9100/9101) lives here, so Payroll is not the destination for practice-performance data.

Rules the selectors enforce

  • Readiness = period records only: unresolved missing time, open pairs, unexplained scheduled days, pending in-period corrections and PTO. Never visibleItems.length === 0.
  • Readiness and export are separate. Prepare report is always available; unresolved issues print on it.
  • A source that is loading, stale, failed, unauthorized, or partial is not empty. Readiness reads can’t confirm with a retry; Home and People say “as of”.
  • Staffing reads Layer 1 only. A snoozed reminder never changes who is clocked in.
  • Priority: a verified active coverage or safety issue outranks any deadline; then deadlines, decisions, fixes, follow-ups; oldest first. Speculative items are never admitted (§5.0 of the document).
  • Deduplication: one item per underlying record condition. A notification, a correction request, an attendance exception, and a payroll issue about the same day are facets of one item, keyed by kind plus record id.
  • Authorization is checked per action against the existing policies (org admin for punch edits and seals; a reviewer who is not the author for versions; nobody signs their own record). The panel hides what the person may not do.
  • Every count is labeled with the subset it counts.

10Interaction model

One rhythm, one queue, one state

The benchmark rhythm

State→Exception→Exact action→Canonical editor→Return→Resolved

Payroll Readiness runs on it, and so does everything else: Attention (row → panel → editor → resolved), People → Today (exception → item), Close the Day follow-up (unsealed day → seal step → sealed), policy review (version in review → review view → approved), bypass follow-up (rule tripped → reasons → note on the record), incidents (awaiting countersign → countersign → closed). No screen states a problem without the exact action beside it.

Attention admission rule · an invariant, not a guideline

An item enters Attention only when one of these is true:

  • the manager must make a decision (a request, a version in review, a challenge to verify, an incident to countersign);
  • a business record is incorrect or incomplete and the manager can fix it (a missing clock-out, a scheduled day with no time, time that looks off, an unsealed or missing closeout);
  • a recorded office rule has reached the point where manager follow-up is required (a record awaiting sign-off, a bypass reason owed past the office's window, an acknowledgment escalated to the manager, training past its due date).

Never admitted: passive observations, weak patterns, FYIs, metrics, "approaching" states, the existence of training, a metric behind pace. Pace lives in a status line; patterns live in People → Patterns as context; overtime lives in Payroll under "worth a look".

Home only navigates

A Home row has exactly one action, Review for decisions and Open for fixes and follow-ups, and both land on the exact Attention item. Home never approves, declines, seals, signs, or edits. That keeps the workbench in one place and keeps Home a five-second read.

Consequential actions confirm; reversals are audited; undo is for UI

Approving PTO writes the ledger and notifies a person. Sealing a day locks a record. Signing off closes a chain. These confirm first with the consequence stated in one sentence. Afterwards the panel offers a reversal only where the system supports one as its own audited action (cancel an approval, unseal a day, edit the day again); a closed record offers none. Ten-second undo applies only to Park and Snooze. Try it below.

Shared selectors, labeled subsets

One set of facts, one selector module. The badge counts needs me now; Home shows the first three of it and names what is waiting or deferred; Payroll counts unresolved payroll issues in the period; the person record separates needs action now from open, deferred. Counts differ because they count different things, and each says which.

Progressive disclosure

A row is one line, a verb, and an age. The panel adds what happened, why the system surfaced it, the deadline, the recorded items behind it, one primary action, and the history. The record has everything. "Why am I seeing this?" names the office rule and the records it read, in the office's words.

Deep linking and return

Every row and every notification opens the exact item at /management?item=<kind>:<id>. Editors accept ?return= and show a return pill; saving returns to where the manager started with the row resolved and an audit line.

Quiet states

Home collapses to a sentence and four lines. Attention reads Nothing waiting · 2 parked until Friday. Payroll reads Ready. No card appears to fill space, no zero becomes a row, no green floods the page, no challenge takes permanent real estate.
